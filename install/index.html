Install Flatball Connect

It's not in the Play Store yet, so it installs from here. It takes a minute, and updates tell you when they're out.

Download for Android

Latest: , released . Android 7 or later.

Step by step

  1. Tap Download for Android above, on your phone. Your browser may warn that the file could be harmful, as it does for any app from outside the Play Store: tap Download anyway.
  2. When it's downloaded, tap Open in the notification, or find flatball.apk in your Files app under Downloads.
  3. The first time, Android asks whether your browser may install apps. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, and go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect says it doesn't know the app, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  5. Open Flatball Connect. Everything in Learn, Train and Watch works with no account; make a free one to join and post games.

Updates

When a new build is out, the app shows a banner on its Learn tab. Download it again from this page and install it over the one you have: your account and everything you've saved stay as they were.
