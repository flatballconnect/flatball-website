Quick Reference · 8 of 12

Strip

The disc got knocked out of a catch

What happens

It's a foul. The offense keeps the disc.

Step by step

  1. A defender contacted the disc after the receiver had control and caused the drop.
  2. If accepted, the receiver keeps possession where they caught it.
  3. If contested, the disc goes back to the thrower.

The hand signal

FoulHold one arm straight out and chop the other forearm across the straight arm

The rules

From the WFDF Rules of Ultimate 2025-2028, word for word.

17.3 Strip Fouls:

17.3.1 A Strip Foul occurs when an opponent fouls a player and that causes the player to drop a disc they caught or to lose possession of the disc.

17.3.2 If the reception would have otherwise been a goal, and the foul is accepted, a goal is awarded.

12.1

A “catch” occurs when a player has a non-spinning disc trapped between at least two body parts. A catch can enable a player to establish possession of the disc.

12.1.1 If the player fails to maintain the catch due to subsequent ground contact related to the catch, or contact related to the catch with a team-mate or a legitimately positioned opposition player, possession is deemed to have not occurred.
